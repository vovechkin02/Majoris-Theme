System Design Notes

Rules reflected throughout the gallery.

4px controls: buttons, entries, tabs and compact selectors.
8px structures: cards, popovers, dialogs and windows.
128ms stationary fades: no button jumping on hover or click.
Window controls are structural: transparent by default, no shadow, red only for close interaction.
Elevation is semantic: shadows show hierarchy, not decoration.

Native document tab contract

40px strip; 14px Medium / SemiBold; 24px close controls.
